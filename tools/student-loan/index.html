Student loan thresholds for 2026/27

The current thresholds for Plans 1, 2, 4, 5 and Postgraduate Loans.

Read article →

Current thresholds

For 2026/27 the annual thresholds are £26,900 for Plan 1, £29,385 for Plan 2, £33,795 for Plan 4, £25,000 for Plan 5 and £21,000 for a Postgraduate Loan.

Repayment rates

Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5 use 9% above the threshold. A Postgraduate Loan uses 6%.

Pay periods matter

Real deductions use weekly or monthly thresholds. An annual estimate can differ from a single payslip.
