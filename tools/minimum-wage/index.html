UK minimum wage rates from April 2026

The current rates for workers aged 21+, 18 to 20, under 18 and apprentices.

Read article →

Rates from 1 April 2026

Age 21 and over is £12.71. Age 18 to 20 is £10.85. Under 18 and the apprentice rate are £8.00.

Apprentice rules

The apprentice rate depends on age and year of apprenticeship.

Not a full pay audit

Unpaid working time, deductions and accommodation can affect minimum-wage compliance.
