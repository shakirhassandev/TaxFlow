What it is

A levy on certain UK oil and gas profits. It has its own detailed rules and time limits.

Why there is no simple calculator here

The amount can depend on details such as local rates, product type, emissions, customs codes, business size or special reliefs. A short calculator would give a false sense of accuracy.
