Other UK taxes and duties

Taxes that usually need specialist data or official tables

These pages explain the tax in plain English and link to the official source. We do not force a calculator where the inputs would be too complex or misleading.

Local tax

Council Tax

A local tax on domestic property. The amount depends on the property band, local authority and any discounts or exemptions.

Business property

Business Rates

A tax on many non-domestic properties. The bill depends on rateable value, the local multiplier and reliefs.

Vehicles

Vehicle Excise Duty

Vehicle tax depends on the vehicle, registration date, emissions, fuel type and other rules.

Insurance

Insurance Premium Tax

A tax included in many insurance premiums. Different rates apply to different types of insurance.

Travel

Air Passenger Duty

A duty paid by airlines for passengers leaving the UK. Rates depend on destination and class of travel.

Fuel

Fuel Duty

Excise duty charged on petrol, diesel and some other fuels. Rates are set by the government.

Alcohol

Alcohol Duty

Duty charged on alcoholic products. The amount depends on the product and alcoholic strength.

Tobacco

Tobacco Duty

Duty charged on tobacco products. Product type and quantity affect the amount.

Imports

Customs Duty

Duty can apply to goods brought into the UK. The rate depends on the goods, origin and customs value.

Environment

Landfill Tax

Tax charged on waste disposed of at landfill sites in England and Northern Ireland. Scotland and Wales have their own landfill taxes.

Energy

Climate Change Levy

A tax on certain energy supplied to businesses and the public sector.

Construction materials

Aggregates Levy

A tax on commercial exploitation of rock, sand and gravel in the UK.

Packaging

Plastic Packaging Tax

A tax on certain plastic packaging components that do not meet the recycled-plastic requirement.

Drinks

Soft Drinks Industry Levy

A levy on producers and importers of certain soft drinks with added sugar.

Employers

Apprenticeship Levy

A levy for employers with a large annual pay bill. It is collected through PAYE.

Shares

Stamp Duty on shares

Stamp Duty or SDRT can apply when buying certain shares and securities.

Property companies

Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings

An annual tax that can apply to companies and some other non-natural persons owning UK residential property above the value threshold.

Large digital groups

Digital Services Tax

A tax aimed at certain large digital businesses with UK user-linked revenue.

Banks

Bank Levy

A levy on certain equity and liabilities of banking groups and building societies.

Oil and gas

Energy Profits Levy

A levy on certain UK oil and gas profits. It has its own detailed rules and time limits.

Gambling

Gambling duties

The UK has several gambling duties, with different rules for betting, gaming and remote gambling.

Wales

Landfill Disposals Tax

The Welsh tax on qualifying landfill disposals. It is administered by the Welsh Revenue Authority.

Scotland

Scottish Landfill Tax

The Scottish tax on taxable landfill disposals. It is administered by Revenue Scotland.
