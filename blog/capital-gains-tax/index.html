Capital Gains Tax in 2026/27

The £3,000 annual exempt amount, the main 18% and 24% rates, and common reasons a gain can be different.

Updated 6 October 2026Tax year 2026/27

The £3,000 annual exempt amount, the main 18% and 24% rates, and common reasons a gain can be different.

The annual exempt amount

For most individuals the annual exempt amount is £3,000 in 2026/27.

Main rates

The main rates for individuals are 18% and 24%. The amount at each rate depends on your taxable income and the size of the gain.

Reliefs can change the answer

Home sales, business disposals, losses and some special assets have extra rules.
