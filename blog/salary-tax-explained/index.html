How salary tax works in the UK

A plain-English look at PAYE, Income Tax, National Insurance and why take-home pay can change.

Updated 6 October 2026Tax year 2026/27

A plain-English look at PAYE, Income Tax, National Insurance and why take-home pay can change.

Start with gross pay

Your employer starts with gross pay, then payroll works out PAYE Income Tax, employee National Insurance and any other deductions.

Your tax code matters

A tax code tells payroll how HMRC wants your allowance handled. Two people on the same salary can have different take-home pay if their codes or deductions are different.

Use the calculator as an estimate

The salary calculator is useful for planning a job move, pay rise or budget. Your actual payslip can differ because payroll works by pay period.

Use the numbers, not guesswork. Open the linked calculator to model your own situation, then check the official rules if the result will be used for a filing or payment decision.
